## What R11C relies on

- Compiling, linking and testing with the historical toolchain (GCC 1.40, GAS 1.38,
  i386-ld): no redistribution.
- Using the compiler-support sources above in a build step, with their notices.
- Reading the C68 and 1993 attachments for behaviour and conventions only.
